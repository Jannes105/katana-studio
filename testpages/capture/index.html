Katana Capture Tool

Records the MIDI traffic between this PC and a BOSS Katana Gen 3 to answer the open protocol questions of "Phase 0 on the PC". Chrome on the PC, amp connected by USB with the BOSS driver, BOSS Tone Studio closed (the port is exclusive on Windows). Unofficial hobby project, not affiliated with BOSS/Roland.

Connection

Not connected.

Connecting only sends an identity request. Editor mode is not switched on or off automatically, not even when the page closes; that is one of the experiments (buttons in "Write experiments").

Recorder

The screen shows the last 500 lines and shortens long messages; the export is complete. Captures may contain personal patch data: keep them in the git-ignored captures/ folder of the repo, never commit them.

Write experiments sends DT1 to the amp

  
  

    
Editor mode (DT1 7F 00 00 01)

    
    

    
NS threshold of the current patch (DT1 20 00 58 01)

    

      
      
    

    
    
Harmless and audible. The change is not saved on the amp unless you save the patch there.


    
PATCH_SELECT (DT1 7F 00 01 00)

    

      
      
    

    
Assumed encoding u2x4, verify: the index goes out as two nibble bytes (00 0n). Only
    indexes 1 to 8 (A1 to B4) are offered. Unsaved changes of the current channel are lost on the amp.


    
Custom DT1

    

      
      
      
    

    
Asks for confirmation. There is deliberately no button for PATCH_WRITE, EXPORT or PREVIEW_MUTE
    in this version: their addresses and semantics are not verified. Do not send them through this field either.
